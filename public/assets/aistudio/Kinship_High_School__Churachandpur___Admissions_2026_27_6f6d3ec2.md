## About Kinship High School

Established in 1994 to educate and uplift children.

Kinship High School was established in 1994 in response to the need to educate and uplift children. Children grow up here in an atmosphere that encourages co-existence, respect for the feelings and beliefs of others, and the breaking down of communal and caste barriers.

The school is run with faith in God and stands under the motto *spiritus, mens, corpus*: a soul that breathes peace, and a body of literature and learning. It provides education from preparatory to Class X and is recognised by BOSEM (No. 68633/2012).

## School rules and policies

Please read these with your child. Parents and guardians are partners in the school’s work.

Fees

School fees are charged for twelve calendar months. Pay in advance or before the 10th of every month. Fees may be revised from time to time.

Attendance and leave

1. A regular student must attend at least 90% of school days.
2. In case of sickness, send an application with a medical certificate and report the cause to the Academic Dean.
3. A student absent for 5 consecutive days in a month without permission will be expelled without prior notice.
4. Students below 90% attendance cannot sit for the promotion exam.

Absence fines

Preparatory–I: Rs. 10 per day\
Class II–V: Rs. 20 per day\
Class VI–VIII: Rs. 30 per day\
Class IX–X: Rs. 50 per day

Do’s for students

1. Come on time, well groomed, in proper uniform and polished shoes.
2. Be regular and never late.
3. Speak in English on the school premises and during travel.
4. Keep classrooms and the premises clean and tidy.
5. Switch off fans and lights after use.
6. Be courteous and respectful to parents, teachers, elders, friends and classmates.
7. Be fair and honest at work.
8. Complete work on time and respect deadlines.
9. Bring proper materials: pen, pencil, paper and books.

Don’ts for students

1. Do not be irregular or bunk classes.
2. Do not use abusive language.
3. Do not damage school property.
4. Do not bring mobiles, iPods, tablets or other gadgets. They are strictly prohibited.
5. Do not wear expensive or gold jewellery.
6. Do not bring chewing gum, or chocolates in bulk.
7. Do not get into physical fights or bullying.
8. Do not sit on tables and desks.
9. Do not make loud noise or create confusion in the school building.
10. Do not eat in class, on the stage or in corridors.
11. Students may not meet parents or guardians during class hours.
12. Boys must keep hair short, above the collar and the ear.

Disciplinary measures

1. Indiscipline, insubordination, interfering in administration, or disrespect for any teacher, religion or community invites immediate action, including suspension or dismissal.
2. Alcohol, drugs, smoking, paan and tobacco are strictly forbidden, anytime and anywhere. The usual action is suspension or dismissal.
3. Forging signatures and stealing are serious offences and may result in suspension or dismissal.
4. Serious misbehaviour that brings discredit to the school can lead to suspension or dismissal, even outside the school premises.
5. Students who leave or are dismissed are not normally re-admitted.
6. Any damage to property must be made good by the student concerned.
7. Meetings, collections, unions or organisations, and class-wise picnics are strictly prohibited.

For parents and guardians

1. Parents may not meet their wards during class hours without permission from the school authority.
2. Parents are always welcome to discuss their child’s progress with the Principal or Vice-Principal.
3. Every fourth child of the same parent pays no monthly fee.

The support of parents is essential to the school’s work. Please send children regularly, on time, in full uniform, with neat books and a tidy appearance.

Awards

1. Top 10 students in the HSLC exam receive Rs. 20,000.
2. Students with letter marks in mathematics and science in the HSLC exam receive Rs. 2,000 per subject.
3. The class topper (Preparatory–IX) of the year receives sponsored tuition fees the next year.

Transfer certificate

A transfer certificate is issued one day after a written request from the parent or guardian, on payment of Rs. 1,000. Students leaving during the term must pay tuition fees for the whole year.

Hostel and boarding rules

1. Arrive on the fixed date. A fine applies for late arrival.
2. Bring your own luggage: Bible, mosquito net, blanket, box, utensils, uniforms, bucket and personal lamp.
3. Speak only English within the compound.
4. Boys and girls may not sit together. Harsh words and a challenging tone toward each other or the wardens are not allowed.
5. Keep pocket money in the office, not with you.
6. The school is not responsible for lost goods. Mark all belongings with your name and registration number. Checks may happen at any time.
7. A medical fee of Rs. 100 per student is compulsory.
8. Quarrelling, fighting, forming parties and inciting communal feelings are forbidden and can lead to suspension or dismissal.
9. No funny dress, long hair for boys, costly ornaments, cosmetics, transparent or thin dresses, eyebrow work, lipstick or rings.
10. Disturbance after lights off, stealing, borrowing or lending things, smoking, tobacco and drinking lead to disciplinary action or dismissal.
11. Love affairs and exchanging love or friendly letters are not allowed.
12. Visitors, friends, relatives and parents may be met only with permission from the head in charge.
13. Sunday devotional service, social work and P.T. are compulsory.
14. The school and hostel are not responsible for accidents to students who go outside the compound without permission.
15. A student who leaves the hostel (except on medical grounds with a doctor’s proof) must pay hostel fees for the whole year.
16. Doors are closed during school hours (8:30 AM–2:30 PM), study hours and chapel services.
17. Late return on the given date and time costs Rs. 100 per day. Everyone must submit the exit form to the warden.

Visiting days and hours

Students may not meet parents and relatives during class hours. Parents and guardians may visit their children every Saturday, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM only.

## Prayers and school anthem

Our prayer

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done; on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation; but deliver us from the evil one. For thine is the kingdom, the power and the glory, for ever and ever. Amen.

School anthem

Behold the Kinshipians’ destinies ahead,\
March on with zeal for the best education.\
Let our light shine across the land,\
From thine blessed Kinship High School,\
Step up each day to learn the truth;\
The truth of God and his wonders.\
Diversification yet here we converge,\
Striving for the best foundation.\
Though trials and foes are around,\
Jesus takes the lead.\
Struggling for the concealed wisdom,\
With spiritus, mens, corpus.\
With our heart and soul we proclaim our motto:\
To train up a child in the way he should go.

Morning prayer

Gracious Heavenly Father, we thank thee for blessing and bringing us together to our school again. Bless our school and beloved teachers, and give us knowledge, wisdom and understanding. We commit this day into thy mighty hand. We pray in the name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. Amen.

Evening prayer

Almighty merciful Father, we thank thee for helping and protecting us throughout the day. We give thanks to thee for watching over and hearing our humble prayer. Lord, lead us safely to our respective homes and bring us back to our school tomorrow. Protect us from all harms and dangers. In Jesus’ name we pray. Amen.

## Frequently asked questions

Which classes are open for admission?

Admissions are open from Preparatory to Class X, subject to seat availability.

What are the school hours?

School hours are 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM. The office is open 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Does the school have a hostel?

Yes. Hostel and boarding facilities are available, with their own rules. See School rules and policies above.

How can I check my child's progress?

Parents are always welcome to discuss their child’s progress with the Principal or Vice-Principal.

Are there awards or fee concessions?

Every fourth child of the same parent pays no monthly fee. Top students receive cash awards, and class toppers (Preparatory–IX) get sponsored tuition fees the next year.
